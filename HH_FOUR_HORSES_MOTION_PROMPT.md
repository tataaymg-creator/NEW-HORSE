## Seedance

```text
HH FOUR HORSES — 15 s, 21:9, one continuous slow-motion tracking shot. The white horse leads a loose herd of three dark horses galloping to the left; the camera travels beside them at the white horse's speed.

REFERENCES
@HH_FOUR_v01 — THE FIRST FRAME: all four horses, their order, the framing, the distance, the light and the environment exactly as this image.
@gallop_side_ref — ONLY the timing of the legs in a gallop; it runs to the left like them. Ignore its body, shape and look.

CAMERA
Tracking vehicle running alongside at exactly the white horse's speed, side-on and level, 50 mm from far away, steady with a faint organic float. The white horse stays locked where the first frame puts him, a little left of centre, with the wide empty dark space on the left, for all 15 s. The sand slides past to the right beneath them. The framing stays as wide as the first frame; the camera never pushes in.

ACTION
0.0–5.0s — The first frame comes alive: four horses at full gallop in deep slow motion, one stride about every 1.8 s, each in its own rhythm, legs never in step, heads rising and dipping at different moments.
5.0–10.0s — The three dark horses drive hard behind him, necks stretched, ears pinned, nostrils wide; the bay stays closest to him at his shoulder, the black and the chestnut shift a little in their places behind. The white horse runs freer, ears forward, mane high.
10.0–15.0s — The white horse slowly draws a little further ahead of the bay, stride by stride; the herd keeps galloping behind him. The shot ends mid-stride.

DUST AND SAND
Every hoof strike throws sand and clods up and back; their dust rises into one long rolling golden wall behind and between their legs, drifting right and up into the sun shafts. All the flying sand comes from their hooves.

LIGHT IN MOTION
Warm golden backlight from high behind them: the rims on their ears, manes, backs and tails flicker as the hair moves. The golden sun shafts from the top shimmer, thicken and thin as the dust rolls through them. Exposure stays even; the white coat never glows.

THE HORSES
The white horse exactly as in the first frame: a clean, almost pure white coat with no freckles. The dark bay, the black and the chestnut exactly as in the first frame: all full-grown adult horses of the same size. All bare, no tack. Always four horses, never more, never fewer.

TEMPO
Real high-speed slow motion at 24 fps, everything slowed equally: legs, manes, tails, sand, dust and light, fluid in every frame.

LOOK
Photoreal live-action commercial. Deep black void, churned brown sand, amber-gold dust, golden sun shafts, deep clean blacks, fine natural grain. 21:9.

SOUND
Slowed, deep, muffled hoofbeats of four horses in a rolling rhythm, breathing, wind, the hiss of thrown sand. No music, no voices.

KEEP CONSISTENT
- The camera tracks the white horse; he stays in the same place in the frame and always leads.
- The order stays: white in front, the bay beside him, the black and the chestnut behind.
- The framing stays wide; the dark space around them stays empty: no walls, no sky, no people.
- One continuous shot, no cuts, no on-screen text.
```
