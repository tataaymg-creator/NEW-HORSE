## 2. Motion — Seedance, 8 s

Start frame: `HH_OUTRO_v01`. Video reference: `gallop_side_ref` (timing of the legs only). 8 s, 21:9.

```text
HH OUTRO — 8 s, 21:9, one continuous slow-motion shot. The white horse gallops straight toward the camera from far away; the golden light behind him grows stronger and stronger until it fills the whole frame.

REFERENCES
@HH_OUTRO_v01 — THE FIRST FRAME: the horse, the framing, the distance, the light and the environment exactly as this image.
@gallop_side_ref — ONLY the timing of the legs in a gallop; ignore its angle, body, shape and look.

CAMERA
The camera travels backward ahead of him at exactly his speed, low at the height of his chest, 50 mm from far away, from the very first frame. He stays the same small size in the centre of the frame for the whole shot; the camera never pushes in and he never comes closer. The sand slides toward the camera beneath him.

ACTION
0.0–4.0s — He gallops straight at the camera in deep slow motion, one stride about every 1.8 s: forelegs reaching and striking the sand, head and neck dipping and rising, his mane streaming back on both sides of his neck. The golden glow behind him is steady.
4.0–6.5s — The light behind him starts to grow: the glow on the horizon brightens and spreads, the sun shafts thicken, the rim around his body burns brighter and wider, the sand around him turns gold.
6.5–8.0s — The light keeps growing until it swallows him and fills the whole frame with warm golden-white light. The last frame is almost pure warm light.

AIR
Clean air for the whole shot: no dust clouds, no blowing sand; only a little sand thrown low by his hooves, falling back at once.

LIGHT
The growing light comes only from behind him: it brightens smoothly and steadily, never flickering, the black void slowly filling with warm light from the horizon outward. His face stays readable until the light swallows him.

THE HORSE
Exactly the horse of the first frame: a clean, almost pure white coat with no freckles, lean and elegant, long mane. Bare, no tack.

TEMPO
Real high-speed slow motion at 24 fps: legs, mane, sand and light all slowed equally, fluid in every frame.

LOOK
Photoreal live-action commercial. Deep black void turning to warm golden light, churned brown sand, deep clean blacks at the start, fine natural grain. 21:9.

SOUND
Slowed, deep, muffled hoofbeats coming toward us, breathing, a rising soft wind as the light grows. No music, no voices.

KEEP CONSISTENT
- He gallops straight at the camera and stays the same size in the centre of the frame.
- No dust clouds at any time.
- The light grows only from behind him and ends filling the frame.
- One horse only, no people; one continuous shot, no on-screen text.
```
