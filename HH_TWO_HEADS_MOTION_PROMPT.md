# HH two horses — motion prompt v01 (15 s, the bay falls behind and leaves)

Start frame: the approved two-horse still, named `HH_TWO_v01`. Video reference: `gallop_side_ref` (it already runs screen-left like them, so no mirroring). 15 s, 21:9.

```text
HH TWO HORSES — 15 s, 21:9, one continuous slow-motion tracking shot. The white horse and the bay gallop side by side to the left; the camera travels beside them at the white horse's speed. In the last seconds the bay falls behind and drops out of the frame on the right, and the white horse runs on alone.

REFERENCES
@HH_TWO_v01 — THE FIRST FRAME: both horses, the framing, the distance, the light and the environment exactly as this image.
@gallop_side_ref — ONLY the timing of the legs in a gallop; it runs to the left like them. Ignore its body, shape and look.

CAMERA
Tracking vehicle running alongside at exactly the white horse's speed, side-on and level, 50 mm from far away, steady with a faint organic float. The white horse stays locked where the first frame puts him, small in the frame, a little left of centre, for all 15 s. The sand floor slides past to the right beneath them. The framing stays as wide as the first frame; the camera never pushes in.

ACTION
0.0–9.0s — Both horses gallop side by side in deep slow motion, one stride about every 1.8 s: the white horse leads, the bay half a length behind him on the far side. Their long manes and tails stream back to the right, legs reaching and striking the sand, sand and clods thrown up behind every hoof.
9.0–12.0s — The bay starts to tire and loses ground: stride by stride he drifts back to the right across the frame, behind the white horse's tail, his head falling behind, until he runs out through the right edge of the frame and is gone. The white horse never slows.
12.0–15.0s — The white horse gallops on alone, locked in the same place in the frame, his mane streaming, the empty dark around him; the bay's dust drifts and settles behind him on the right.

DUST AND SAND
Every hoof strike throws sand and clods up and back; a warm trail of dust rolls up behind them and drifts to the right into the dark. All the flying sand comes from their hooves.

LIGHT IN MOTION
Warm golden backlight from high behind them: the rim on their ears, manes, backs and tails flickers as the hair moves. The golden sun shafts from the top shimmer, thicken and thin as their dust rolls through them. Exposure stays even; the white coat never glows.

THE HORSES
The white horse exactly as in the first frame: a clean, almost pure white coat with no freckles, lean and elegant, long mane. The bay exactly as in the first frame: dark bay with a black mane and tail. Both bare, no tack. They never change.

TEMPO
Real high-speed slow motion at 24 fps, everything slowed equally: legs, manes, tails, sand, dust and light, fluid in every frame.

LOOK
Photoreal live-action commercial. Deep black void, churned brown sand, amber-gold dust, golden sun shafts, deep clean blacks, fine natural grain. 21:9.

SOUND
Slowed, deep, muffled hoofbeats of two horses, thinning to one horse in the last 3 s; breathing, wind, the hiss of thrown sand. No music, no voices.

KEEP CONSISTENT
- The camera tracks the white horse; he stays in the same place in the frame and never slows.
- The bay leaves only by falling behind to the right; he never passes the white horse.
- The framing stays wide; the dark space around them stays empty: no walls, no sky, no people.
- One continuous shot, no cuts, no on-screen text.
```
