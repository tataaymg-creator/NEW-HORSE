# HH intro — empty frame, he crosses left to right and leaves

How: 1) make the empty plate still; 2) animate it in Seedance with the plate as the start frame (and as the end frame too, if the end-frame slot is available), the horse character sheet as an image reference and `gallop_side_ref.mp4` as the video reference.

## 1. Still — empty plate (save as `HH_INTRO_v01.png`)

```text
Photoreal empty frame, locked-off side view at the height of a horse's withers, 50 mm lens: a deep black void with no walls, no horizon and no sky. A floor of loose, churned brown sand runs across the bottom quarter of the frame, catching a warm golden backlight. Warm sun shafts fall from high behind through drifting dust; faint gold dust hangs in the air across the middle of the frame. The middle half of the frame is open dark space, ready for a horse to run through. Deep clean blacks, amber-gold dust, fine natural grain. 21:9.
```

## 2. Motion — 8 s

```text
HH INTRO — 8 s, 21:9, one continuous slow-motion shot, locked-off camera. The empty frame; the white horse gallops in from the left edge, crosses the whole frame without slowing and leaves through the right edge; the frame ends empty again.

REFERENCES
@HH_INTRO_v01 — THE FIRST FRAME: the empty plate, framing, light and environment exactly as this image.
@HH_horse_sheet — the horse: exactly this white horse.
@gallop_side_ref — ONLY the gallop: leg mechanics, the rise and fall of the body and the tempo. It runs screen-left; mirror it, he runs screen-right. Ignore its look and background.

CAMERA
Locked off on a heavy tripod, side view at the height of his withers, 50 mm lens. No pan, no tilt, no zoom: the frame never follows him.

THE HORSE
Full side view, his whole body in the middle half of the frame height: ears near the top of that band, hooves on the sand at its bottom. His long mane streams back and up in the wind, his neck visible under it; his tail streams back.

ACTION
0.0–1.5s — The empty frame: dust drifts slowly through the sun shafts.
1.5–2.5s — His head and streaming mane enter from the left edge, then his whole body, at a full slow-motion gallop.
2.5–5.0s — He crosses the frame without slowing, about one stride every 1.8 s; each hoof strike throws sand and clods, dust rolls up behind him.
5.0–6.0s — He runs out through the right edge, his tail last.
6.0–8.0s — The frame is empty again: the dust cloud he left behind rolls, thins and drifts through the light.

LIGHT IN MOTION
Warm golden backlight rims his mane, back and legs as he passes; the sun shafts shimmer and thicken as his dust rolls through them. His coat never glows.

TEMPO
Real high-speed slow motion at 24 fps, the same tempo as the rest of the film.

LOOK
Photoreal live-action commercial. Deep black void, churned brown sand, amber-gold dust, deep clean blacks, white coat with full texture, fine natural grain. 21:9.

SOUND
Slowed muffled hoofbeats coming in from the left and fading out to the right, breathing, wind, hiss of sand. No music, no voices.

KEEP CONSISTENT
- The camera never moves; only he moves, left to right.
- He never stops or turns; he leaves the frame completely.
- One horse only, no people; one continuous shot, no on-screen text.
```
