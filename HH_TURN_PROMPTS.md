## Motion — 10 s, he turns before the right edge and gallops back, tracked

Start frame: the approved still, `HH_TURN_v01`. Image reference: `HH_horse_sheet`. Video reference: `gallop_side_ref`. 10 s, 21:9.

### Seedance

```text
HH TURN — 10 s, 21:9, one continuous slow-motion tracking shot from far away. The white horse gallops to the right; just before he reaches the right edge of the frame he turns around and gallops back to the left, his tail toward the right edge. The camera tracks him the whole time.

REFERENCES
@HH_TURN_v01 — THE FIRST FRAME: the horse, the framing, the distance, the light and the environment exactly as this image.
@HH_horse_sheet — the horse: his face, head and body exactly as this character sheet, in every frame.
@gallop_side_ref — ONLY the timing of the legs in a gallop. Ignore its body, shape and look.

CAMERA
Side-on and level at the height of his withers, 50 mm from far away, on a tracking vehicle that is already moving from the first frame. It follows him to the right a little slower than he runs, so he slowly gains ground toward the right side of the frame; when he turns, the camera eases to a stop and then tracks back to the left with him. He stays far away and small for the whole shot, the same size as in the first frame. No zoom, no push-in.

ACTION
0.0–3.5s — He gallops to the right in deep slow motion, one stride about every 1.8 s, his mane streaming back, slowly moving from the centre toward the right side of the frame.
3.5–6.5s — About three quarters of the way across, before he reaches the right edge, he turns around: he gathers himself, his hind legs dig into the sand, and he swings round in a wide, powerful U-turn toward the camera, his head and neck leading the turn, his mane and tail swinging out wide, sand spraying from his hooves in an arc. For a moment we see him from the front.
6.5–10.0s — He has turned completely: his tail is toward the right edge of the frame and he gallops away to the left in clean profile, his mane streaming back to the right. The camera tracks with him to the left; he stays the same small size.

DUST AND SAND
Every hoof strike throws sand; the turn throws a big arc of sand and a golden dust cloud that drifts and settles behind him. All the flying sand comes from his hooves.

LIGHT IN MOTION
Warm golden backlight from high behind him: the rim on his mane, back and tail flickers as he moves and turns. The golden sun shafts from the top shimmer as his dust rolls through them. His coat never glows.

THE HORSE
Exactly the horse in @HH_horse_sheet: his face, a clean, almost pure white coat, lean and elegant, long mane and tail. Bare, no tack. He never changes.

TEMPO
Real high-speed slow motion at 24 fps: legs, mane, tail, sand, dust and light all slowed equally, fluid in every frame. The turn is smooth and heavy, never sudden.

LOOK
Photoreal live-action commercial. Deep black void, churned brown sand, amber-gold dust, golden sun shafts, deep clean blacks, fine natural grain. 21:9.

SOUND
Slowed, deep, muffled hoofbeats, a heavy skid of sand in the turn, breathing. No music, no voices.

KEEP CONSISTENT
- He stays far away and small; the camera never pushes in.
- He turns before the right edge, never leaving the frame; after the turn his tail is toward the right edge and he gallops left.
- One horse only, no people; one continuous shot, no on-screen text.
```
